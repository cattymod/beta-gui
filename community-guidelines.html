Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 30th 2026

Welcome to the CattyMod community! 🐱

This is a place for CattyMod users to share ideas, discuss projects, and connect with other creators. Please be considerate of others and help keep the community enjoyable for everyone.

1. Be Kind and Respectful

Everyone deserves to be treated with respect. Be friendly, accept that people have different opinions, and remember that there's a real person behind every post.

  • Be polite, even when you disagree.
  • Don't bully, harass, threaten, or target other members.
  • Respect other people's identities, opinions, and creative ideas.
  • Don't deliberately start arguments or provoke others.

2. Keep Things Appropriate

CattyMod is intended for a general audience, including younger creators. Keep posts suitable for people of different ages.

  • Don't share sexually explicit or graphic content.
  • Don't post hateful, threatening, or discriminatory material.
  • Don't encourage dangerous or harmful activities.
  • Use good judgment when sharing links, images, and other media.

3. Avoid Spam

Keep the community useful and enjoyable to browse.

  • Don't flood the Padlet with repetitive posts.
  • Don't repeatedly advertise unrelated projects or websites.
  • Don't post misleading links or deceptive promotions.
  • Give discussions room to breathe instead of posting excessively.

4. Respect Other Creators

Creativity is what brings many people to CattyMod. Respect the effort that goes into other people's work.

  • Don't claim someone else's project or artwork as your own.
  • Give credit when using or sharing another creator's work.
  • Respect creators' requests about how their work is used.
  • Offer constructive feedback rather than insults.

5. Protect Your Privacy

Think carefully before sharing information online.

  • Never post passwords or account credentials.
  • Don't share someone else's private information without permission.
  • Avoid sharing your address, phone number, or other sensitive details.
  • Be cautious of suspicious links and requests for personal information.

6. No Scams or Malicious Content

Be honest with other community members and help keep the community safer.

  • Don't attempt to scam, defraud, or trick other users.
  • Don't share links designed to steal accounts or information.
  • Don't distribute malware or encourage people to install it.
  • Don't impersonate other people to mislead the community.

7. Remember That This Is a Community

The CattyMod community is hosted on Padlet, an external platform. Members participate using Padlet's available features.

CattyMod provides a link to this community, but the community isn't a built-in feature of the CattyMod website.

Please also follow Padlet's Terms of Service and any other applicable platform rules.

8. Moderation and Reports

The CattyMod community is not actively managed by CattyMod. Posts and interactions take place on Padlet, where moderation and reporting depend on the platform's available features and controls.

Please use Padlet's available reporting tools for content that violates its rules. The CattyMod website owner does not promise to review every post, respond to every report, remove content, or resolve disputes.

If you encounter inappropriate content, use the reporting options available on the platform rather than engaging with or escalating the situation.

9. Use the Community at Your Own Discretion

Community posts are created by users and may not represent the views of CattyMod or its website owner.

Be thoughtful about what you share, which links you open, and how you interact with other members. Padlet's own policies and moderation systems apply to activity on its platform.

10. Changes to These Guidelines

These guidelines may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to CattyMod or its community. If they change, this page may be updated with a revised date.

11. Project Creation Requirements

Projects shared in the CattyMod community must be created primarily for CattyMod or Scratch.

The following requirements apply:

  • Projects must be made using the official CattyMod editor, the official Scratch editor, or an alternative non-block-based development environment that produces Scratch-compatible projects.
  • Projects made using Scratch mods, forks, or derivatives with modified block-based editors are not permitted.
  • Non-block-based programming tools, external editors, compilers, and other development utilities are allowed, provided they are used to create projects intended for CattyMod or Scratch.
  • Projects created using alternative development tools or compilers must be tested in CattyMod to ensure they work correctly.
  • Simply converting or importing a project originally made for an unrelated platform into Scratch or CattyMod does not automatically make it eligible.

The purpose of this rule is to allow creators to use more advanced development tools while keeping all submitted projects within the CattyMod and Scratch ecosystem.

Thanks for being part of CattyMod! 💙

Be creative, be considerate, and enjoy exploring what the community has to offer!
