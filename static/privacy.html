Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

CattyMod is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect personal information from users.

What CattyMod Does

CattyMod allows users to load Scratch projects using project URLs or .sb3 files and provides additional features.

Information We Collect

  • CattyMod does not collect names, email addresses, locations, or other personal information.
  • CattyMod does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or profiling systems.
  • CattyMod does not store user-created projects on its own servers.

Scratch Projects

When loading projects from Scratch, requests may be made directly to Scratch services. In those cases, Scratch's own privacy practices apply.

Learn more: Scratch Privacy Policy

Cloud Variables

Projects using cloud variables communicate with TurboWarp's cloud servers.

  • Your inputted username may be transmitted when required by a project.
  • Project IDs may be temporarily processed to support cloud functionality.
  • CattyMod does not store this information.

Third-Party Services

Some projects, custom extensions, or packaged applications may connect to third-party websites, APIs, or services.

CattyMod does not control these services and is not responsible for their privacy practices, content, or availability.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
